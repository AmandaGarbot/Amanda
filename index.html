About Amanda

Amanda Grabot is a creative marketing strategist with over a decade of experience helping authors, publishers, and brands amplify their voice. Her passion lies at the intersection of storytelling and strategic promotion — crafting campaigns that don't just sell books, but build lasting author brands.

From launching New York Times bestselling authors to helping indie writers find their audience, Amanda brings a premium, detail-oriented approach to every project. She believes every book deserves a campaign as unique as its story.

Based in New York, Amanda works with clients worldwide, blending cinematic aesthetics with data-driven marketing to produce unforgettable results.

— Amanda Grabot
