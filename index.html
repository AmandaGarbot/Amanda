About Amanda

Amanda Grabot is a creative book promotion specialist dedicated exclusively to helping authors bring their stories to life through visual storytelling, audiobook production, and digital marketing. Working closely with authors across different genres, Amanda focuses on creating cinematic book trailers, engaging audiobook experiences, targeted promotional campaigns, and social media marketing strategies designed to help books reach wider audiences and create stronger reader engagement. With a passion for storytelling and audience connection, Amanda combines creativity, emotional presentation, and modern promotional techniques to help authors showcase the heart of their books in a professional and compelling way

— Amanda Grabot
